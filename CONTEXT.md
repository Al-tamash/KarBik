# AutoBazaar

An online marketplace where dealers advertise cars for sale and buyers discover, compare, and inquire about them. Trust and monetisation features (verification, reviews, subscriptions, boosts) sit on top of this core exchange.

## Language

### People

**Dealer**:
A user who lists cars for sale, manages inquiries, and pays for plans/boosts.
_Avoid_: seller, vendor, owner

**Buyer**:
A user who browses listings, saves wishlists, and sends inquiries.
_Avoid_: customer, user (unqualified), visitor

### Marketplace

**Listing**:
A single car advertised for sale by a dealer; the central entity of the marketplace.
_Avoid_: car ad, post, car (when referring to the advertisement rather than the vehicle)

**Inquiry**:
A buyer's contact request expressing interest in a specific listing.
_Avoid_: lead form, message, enquiry

**Lead**:
An inquiry once the dealer starts tracking it through their sales pipeline (status, priority, follow-ups). Every lead starts as an inquiry; not every inquiry becomes an actively worked lead.
_Avoid_: inquiry (once CRM tracking has begun), prospect

**Wishlist**:
A buyer's saved set of listings for later revisit.
_Avoid_: favourites, shortlist

### Monetisation

**Plan**:
A subscription tier defining what a dealer may do (listing limits, boosts per month, feature flags).
_Avoid_: package, tier

**Subscription**:
A dealer's active period on a plan, with start/end dates and status.
_Avoid_: membership, account

**Boost**:
A paid, time-boxed promotion that raises a listing's visibility above regular listings.
_Avoid_: featured (as a noun), promotion, spotlight

### Trust

**Verification**:
The process by which a dealer submits documents to prove their business is genuine; verified dealers display a badge.
_Avoid_: KYC (except when referring to the document itself)

**Review**:
A buyer's public star rating and comment about a dealer.
_Avoid_: feedback, testimonial (the marketing homepage section is a Testimonial; a Review belongs to a dealer's record)

**Inspected Listing**:
A listing backed by a documented inspection report of the vehicle's condition.
_Avoid_: certified (too vague), checked

**RC Verified**:
A listing whose Registration Certificate document has been verified as legitimate.
_Avoid_: papers clear, documents done
