# Homepage (app/page.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Hyderabad used-car buyers, all segments, mostly on phones; secondary: dealers. Job: find a car fast and reach KarBik support. Proof on hand: ~50 real listings, 3 real verified dealers, RC checks. Constraints: no total-count bragging, no duplicate car rails, no invented claims, no generated hub photos.

## Direction contract

THESIS: KarBik's homepage is the category standard done properly: a working marketplace, search and real cars first, explanation last. It refuses the editorial landing page (big headline + paragraph + decorative sections) the current page ships.

OWN-WORLD: White surfaces on a cool #F7F8FA ground, one brand blue #1B6EF3 for identity, links and selected states, deep orange #C2410C for the single primary action per view, success green only on true "RC checked". Archivo for headings, Inter for UI. 16px card radius, hairline borders, soft card shadow. Photos carry all colour.

STORY: The visitor sees Hyderabad, a search box and price-banded shortcuts, then real cars with big prices; understands that KarBik support connects them with the dealer; taps a car or WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header: logo, Hyderabad city chip, wide search field, Buy car / Dealers / Wishlist / auth. Category strip below (Budget, Brand, Body type, Fuel, Transmission). Hero left (≈55%): headline "Used cars from Hyderabad dealers" at 56–60px, a search card with tabs (By budget / By brand / By body type), chips, orange "Show cars". Hero right: real boosted-listing photo with a price tag linking to it. The first listing rail starts within the fold on desktop.

FORM: Category standard (canon), chosen by the user over the roll; quality bar Cars24, Spinny, CarDekho. seed:748edf392975ce31

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
